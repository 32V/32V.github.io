Visual AI Group

Generative AI & Computer Graphics

I am an M.S. student at the KAIST Visual AI Group, advised by Prof. Minhyuk Sung. My research focuses on diffusion and flow-based generative models for multimodal generation, visual content creation, and computer graphics.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science, with a minor in Mathematical Sciences, from KAIST in 2026. I graduated summa cum laude and received the KAIST President’s Award for Outstanding Graduates.
